Delete your data

Smart Commuter Assistant+ — no account is required, but every install has an anonymous device profile you can delete.

Fastest way — in the app:
  1. Open Smart Commuter Assistant+
  2. Go to the Profile tab
  3. Tap Delete my data (or Delete Account if you created one)
  4. Confirm

This takes effect immediately.

Can't open the app?

Email us from the address you'd like removed or, for anonymous profiles, any message describing the request. We process deletion requests within 30 days, usually much sooner.

Email a deletion request Read the Privacy Policy

What gets deleted

  • Your anonymous device profile and account (if you created one), including email and login credentials
  • Your ML-training consent record and trip feedback
  • Your contributed crowd and delay reports are unlinked from your identity and retained in anonymised form, so community crowd forecasts stay accurate for other riders
